01 — THE STUDIO

Most photographs are taken.
A few are waited for.

I work slowly, mostly in natural light, with as little equipment as the day will allow. Fifteen years of weddings, campaigns and covers has taught me the same thing: the frame that lasts is rarely the one that was planned. I would rather come back tomorrow than fake a sunset.
